# SearchDNA

SearchDNA is a demand intelligence platform that turns search, consumption, and market signals into measurable market insight.

SearchDNA is not a traditional keyword tool. It analyzes observable evidence of human intent, curiosity, pain, desire, comparison, and purchase interest.

## Core Metrics

- **Demand Intensity** — How much the market cares
- **Demand Velocity** — How quickly demand is growing or declining
- **Demand Gap** — The difference between demand and available content or solutions
- **Demand Capture** — How much of that demand a brand currently owns

## Planned Data Sources

- Google Trends
- YouTube consumption signals
- Search engine results pages
- Reddit
- TikTok
- Amazon
- Reviews
- Support and customer feedback

## Project Status

SearchDNA is currently in the early architecture and module-specification phase.

## Vision

Turn scattered expressions of demand into clear, actionable intelligence for content strategy, product development, market research, and growth.

## Relationship to Vault

SearchDNA is a demand-intelligence module within the broader Vault ecosystem. It is not a separate cross-project skill warehouse.
